# The Longevity Cake - DRAFT

## A Baked Alternative to the Shake

**Status:** Draft concept - needs testing and refinement

This is a baked version of the meal replacement protocol, designed to:
- Allow proper cooking of sweet potato and teff
- Create resistant starch (forms when cooled after cooking)
- Improve beta-carotene bioavailability through heat
- Enable weekly batch preparation
- Provide the "chew" factor for satiety

---

## Concept: Hybrid Approach

**Baked Component (make weekly):** The cake contains all heat-stable ingredients

**Daily Shake Component:** Protein powder + soy milk + nutritional yeast + amla + kale (preserves heat-sensitive nutrients)

---

## Nutrient Considerations

### What Improves with Baking
- Vitamin A/Beta-carotene: More bioavailable
- Resistant starch: Forms when cooled
- Teff digestibility: Antinutrients reduced
- Mineral absorption: May improve

### What's Lost (and How We Address It)
| Nutrient | Loss | Solution |
|----------|------|----------|
| Vitamin C | 30-50% | Add amla to daily shake (not baked) |
| Folate | 40-50% | Extra lentils + raw kale in shake |
| Thiamine (B1) | 20-40% | Nutritional yeast in shake (not baked) |
| Other B vitamins | 5-25% | Nutritional yeast in shake |

### What Stays Stable
- Flaxseed omega-3 ALA (~100% retention at 350°F)
- All minerals (100%)
- Protein (95%+)
- Fiber (100%)
- Fat-soluble vitamins A, E, K (90%+)
- Niacin B3 (95%)

## BAKED CAKE RECIPE (Makes 7 daily portions)

| Ingredient | Amount | Purpose |

## Instructions

### Prep (Night Before or Morning)
1. **Cook sweet potatoes:** Bake at 400°F for 45-60 min until soft, OR microwave 10-12 min. Let cool enough to handle, then scoop out flesh.
2. **Cook lentils:** If using dry, cook 1 cup dry lentils until soft. OR use 2 cans, drained and rinsed.

### Make the Batter
1. Preheat oven to **325°F (165°C)** - lower temp preserves more nutrients
2. In large bowl, mash sweet potato flesh until smooth
3. Add pitted dates and mash/blend into sweet potato
4. Add cooked lentils and mash (some texture is fine)
5. Add water/soy milk and mix
6. In separate bowl, combine all dry ingredients and spices
7. Add dry to wet, mix until just combined (don't overmix)
8. Fold in chopped Brazil nuts

### Bake
1. Grease a 9x13 inch baking pan (or two 8x8 pans)
2. Spread batter evenly - it will be thick
3. Bake at 325°F for **45-55 minutes** until set and edges pull away
4. A toothpick should come out mostly clean
5. Let cool completely in pan

### Store
1. Cut into 7 equal portions
2. Wrap individually or store in airtight container
3. Refrigerate for up to 7 days
4. Can freeze portions for longer storage

## DAILY SHAKE COMPONENT

To preserve heat-sensitive nutrients, blend this fresh each day:

| Ingredient | Amount | Purpose |
|------------|--------|---------|
| Vivo Life Perform protein | 3 scoops | Protein (doesn't bake well) |
| Soy milk (unsweetened) | 2 cups | Liquid, protein, calcium |
| Baby kale | 3 cups (200g) | Raw folate, calcium, vitamin A |
| Nutritional yeast | 2.5 tbsp | B vitamins (preserved raw) |
| Amla powder | 1 tsp | Vitamin C (preserved raw) |
| Frozen broccoli (thawed) | 1/2 cup | Sulforaphane |
| Water | As needed | Consistency |

**Blend until smooth.** Drink alongside or between cake portions.

## ADVANTAGES OF CAKE FORMAT

1. **Batch prep:** Make once, eat all week
2. **Resistant starch:** Forms when cooled - feeds gut bacteria
3. **Better vitamin A absorption:** Heat increases beta-carotene bioavailability
4. **Teff benefits:** Properly cooked, antinutrients reduced
5. **Chewing:** Activates cephalic phase digestion, improves satiety
6. **Portable:** Wrap and take to work
7. **Variety:** Different texture than shake

## DISADVANTAGES

1. **Some B vitamin/folate loss:** Mitigated by raw shake component
2. **Prep time:** Initial baking takes ~1.5 hours (but saves daily time)
3. **Texture unknown:** May need testing to optimize
4. **Two components:** Must make both cake and shake

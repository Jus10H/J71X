## Why These Brands?

### Heavy Metal Concerns
| Product | Concern | Safe Brand |
|---------|---------|------------|
| Nutritional Yeast | Lead detected in Frontier, KAL, Whole Foods brands | Sari Foods (no detectable lead) |
| Turmeric | Lead contamination common in South Asian imports | American Turmeric (US-grown) or Spice Profile |
| Amla | Potential heavy metals | Pure Indian Foods (lab tested, low-heat) |
| Flaxseed | Cadmium varies widely by brand | Terrasoul (tested, results available) |
| All spices | Heavy metals vary by source | Starwest Botanicals (ICP-MS tested), Spice Profile |

## WEEKLY PREP: Bryan Johnson's Super Veggie Integration

Prepare this as a separate dish 2-3x per week for variety and additional nutrients. Can replace one shake serving.

**Ingredients (per batch):**
- 45g black lentils (dry)
- 250g broccoli (head + stalk)
- 150g cauliflower
- 50g shiitake/maitake mushrooms
- 1 clove garlic
- 3g fresh ginger
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp cumin
- Juice of 1 lime
- Hemp seeds (topping)

**Instructions:**
1. Boil lentils 18-20 min until al dente. Drain and rinse.
2. Boil vegetables with ginger and garlic 7-9 min until tender.
3. Blend all with vinegar, cumin, and lime until thick soup consistency.
4. Top with hemp seeds.

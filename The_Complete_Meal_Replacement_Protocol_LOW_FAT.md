## BLENDING INSTRUCTIONS

**Prep the night before:**
1. If batch-cooking sweet potatoes, have one ready in fridge

**Morning blend order:**

1. **Liquids:** Soy milk + 1 cup water + blackstrap molasses
2. **Sweet potato:** Scooped flesh (cooked, cooled)
3. **Soft items:** Pitted dates, frozen mango, frozen berries
4. **Legumes:** Drained/rinsed lentils and black beans (or eat separately)

6. **Dry ingredients:** Oats, sorghum flour, protein powder, wheat germ

## BATCH PREP: SWEET POTATOES

**Option A: Microwave (daily)**
1. Pierce sweet potato with fork several times
2. Microwave on high 5-8 minutes until soft
3. Let cool slightly, scoop flesh into blender

**Option B: Batch bake (weekend)**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F
2. Pierce 7 sweet potatoes with fork
3. Place on baking sheet lined with foil
4. Bake 45-60 minutes until soft
5. Let cool, then refrigerate
6. Each morning, scoop cold flesh into blender (cold = more resistant starch!)

## NOTES

1. **Verify in Cronometer:** Input these exact ingredients to confirm macros and micros

2. **Blackstrap molasses:** Take separately (2 tbsp) or add to shake. Safe for healthy adults. Provides iron and potassium in addition to calcium. Note: diabetics or those with kidney disease should consult a doctor.

3. **Frozen mango:** Non-organic is safe - mango is on EWG's "Clean Fifteen" list. Use frozen year-round for convenience and vitamin E.

4. **Protein adjustment:** If you want more protein, add 4th scoop of Clean Protein (+21g protein, +1.6g fat)

5. **Resistant starch bonus:** Using cold (refrigerated) sweet potato increases resistant starch content - better for gut bacteria

## Project Overview

This is a nutrition research and meal planning project (not a software codebase). The goal is to develop an optimized plant-based meal replacement shake based on longevity research and whole-food nutrition principles.

## Core Requirements

The 13 guiding principles in `/requirements`:
1. Minimally processed ingredients
2. 100% plant-based
3. Micronutrient focused
4. Low fat, near-zero saturated fat (carb/protein emphasis for weight lifting)
5. No artificial flavors or sweeteners
6. EU-compliant ingredients
7. Third-party tested supplements
8. Must taste good (problematic ingredients like algae oil go in separate capsules)
9. Science-backed approaches from plant-based doctors and longevity researchers
10. No oil
11. Supplements must have studies showing isolate effectiveness
12. Incorporate Bryan Johnson's Blueprint protocol where possible
13. Quality over cost

## Final Protocol Summary

~2,100 calories across 3 servings with 130-157g protein (varies by version).

**Key components:**
- **Base**: Rolled oats, sorghum flour, Japanese sweet potato (or banana for no-cook), lentils
- **Protein**: Vivo Life Perform (pea/hemp), soy milk
- **Fats**: Ground flaxseed (Esselstyn-approved), walnuts, Brazil nuts (reduced in low-fat)
- **Greens**: Kale/collards, broccoli, cauliflower
- **Superfoods**: Amla, nutritional yeast, turmeric, black cumin, ginger, cumin, garlic
- **Supplements**: Creatine (Thorne), Algae omega-3 (Nordic Naturals), B12, D3+K2, Iodine
- **"Chew" foods**: Raw carrots, bell peppers, apples

**Protocols integrated:**
- Dr. Greger's Daily Dozen (100%)
- Dr. Greger's 21 Tweaks (15-18 of 21)
- Dr. Greger's Anti-Aging 8 (100%)
- Bryan Johnson's Blueprint / Super Veggie
- Dr. Esselstyn's heart disease reversal guidelines (low-fat version)

# The Complete Meal Replacement Protocol - NO COOK VERSION

## For Busy Days - Zero Cooking Required

This version requires **no cooking, roasting, boiling, or steaming**. Everything is either:
- Raw and blendable
- Pre-cooked (canned)
- Soaked overnight
- Frozen (pre-blanched)

**Active prep time: 5 minutes**

---

## Key Substitutions from Standard Protocol

| Standard Protocol | No-Cook Version | Reason |
|-------------------|-----------------|--------|
| Japanese Sweet Potato (cooked) | **Frozen Banana + Extra Oats** | Sweet potato requires roasting/steaming |
| Red Lentils (cooked) | **Canned Lentils** | Just drain and rinse |
| Black Lentils (cooked) | **Canned Black Beans** | Pre-cooked, similar nutrition |
| Fresh Kale (blanched) | **Baby Kale or Baby Spinach** | Tender enough to blend raw |
| Fresh Broccoli | **Frozen Broccoli Florets** | Pre-blanched, thaw in fridge overnight |
| Shiitake Mushrooms (cooked) | **REMOVED** | Requires cooking for safety |

---

## The Night Before (2 minutes)

1. **Move frozen broccoli to fridge** (1/2 cup) - will thaw overnight
2. **Move frozen cauliflower to fridge** (1/2 cup) - will thaw overnight
3. **Optional: Soak oats** in water or soy milk for creamier texture (not required - rolled oats blend fine dry)

## BLENDING INSTRUCTIONS

**Equipment:** High-speed blender (Vitamix, Blendtec, or Ninja)

**Order of ingredients (for best blending):**

1. **Liquids first:** Soy milk + 1 cup water
2. **Soft items:** Thawed frozen banana chunks, pitted dates
3. **Canned legumes:** Drained/rinsed lentils and black beans
4. **Greens:** Baby kale, thawed broccoli & cauliflower
5. **Dry ingredients:** Oats, sorghum flour, protein powder
6. **Fats:** Flaxseed, walnuts, Brazil nuts, hemp seeds
7. **Powders last:** All spices, amla, nutritional yeast

**Blend on high for 60-90 seconds** until completely smooth.

**Add more water** to reach desired consistency (total ~1.5-2 liters).

## TIPS FOR SUCCESS

1. **Freeze bananas in advance**: When bananas get spotty, peel, break into chunks, freeze in ziplock bags. Always have a supply ready.

2. **Thaw frozen veg overnight**: Move broccoli/cauliflower from freezer to fridge before bed. By morning, perfectly thawed.

3. **Rinse canned legumes well**: Reduces sodium by 40% and removes the "canned" taste.

4. **Blend oats first**: For smoothest texture, pulse oats alone for 10 seconds to make flour, then add everything else.

5. **Pre-portion dry ingredients**: On Sunday, measure out 7 days of oats + sorghum + spices into individual containers. Grab and blend.

6. **Buy pre-washed greens**: Baby kale in bags = zero prep.
